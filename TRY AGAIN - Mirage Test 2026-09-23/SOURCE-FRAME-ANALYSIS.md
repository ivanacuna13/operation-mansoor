## What v1 got wrong
Sparse sampling missed temporal design. The triptych was not an interpretation of recursive silhouettes. A clean brush-style font was not an eroded reveal. Black DO held on white inverted the source’s main impact. Small contained footage with giant dead margins did not preserve the relationship of subject, light and space. Reused clips and a known wrong-person clip violated the B-roll rules. These are authoring and selection failures, not evidence that Tesseract cannot do the work.

## Rebuild constraints
Exact original AAC throughout; no sound additions. Single causal arc for a Lone Wolf viewer: private doubt → another deliberate effort → showing up → question still open. No invented outcome, chronology or biography. Real Mansoor in every footage beat; no family or ambiguous identity, no luxury inserted as hardship, no recycled source across beats. Native editable typography, bounded texture erosion and reveal; no generated lettering. Portrait-specific framing and text clear of face. Effects must serve the beat: inward echo, interrupted effort, DO aperture, light that expands into action, then recedes.
