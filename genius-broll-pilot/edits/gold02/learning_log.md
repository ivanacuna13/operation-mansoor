# Gold 02 learning log

## What worked

- Treating the already-winning sound as fixed eliminated unnecessary “moment selection.”
- Keeping the original performance continuously on V1 prevented the slideshow problem and protected Mansoor's strongest facial delivery.
- Four selective B-roll inserts were enough to build a story without covering every line.
- Mansoor-only moving footage made the edit feel like his story rather than generic motivation.
- The strongest emotional route for this sound is control of state and time, with money as consequence rather than the hero.

## What broke and was corrected

- The earlier pilots overused stills, generic emotional shorthand, and invented family meaning. Those versions are invalid references.
- The first Gold 02 visual pass used a variable font that rendered too lightly. The overlay was rebuilt with a true bold face.
- The first local reference graph did not offset B-roll clips onto the master timeline. Explicit `setpts` offsets corrected the preview so it now mirrors the XML placements.
- Premiere UI control was the wrong workflow. The handoff is generated FCP7/XMEML, consistent with the other editing sessions.

## Footage still missing

- A current, premium, moving main-stage shot of Mansoor would improve the authority beat; the available podium clip reads older and more modest.
- More current moving footage that clearly communicates peaceful schedule control—not merely travel—would give future pilots a stronger payoff.
- Additional clean, vertical clips of Mansoor leading a team or speaking to a substantial audience would reduce repetition across the backlog.

## Next human-feedback question

Does the emotional arc read as “I control my state and therefore my time,” or does the podium-to-beach transition still feel too generic? The answer should guide the next cut before any skill is codified.
