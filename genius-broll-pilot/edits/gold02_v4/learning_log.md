# Gold 02 v4 learning log

## What broke in v3

- The pacing improved, but a source clip appeared more than once. A different time range is still repetition to the viewer.
- Several environments felt café-heavy, reducing the sense of story progression.
- The text system was technically animated but visually elementary: small caption cards and word pills did not create editorial emphasis.
- Treating captions as readable subtitles missed the reference's real mechanism: typography is part of the visual edit.

## What v4 changes

- Nine story beats now use nine different Drive source IDs and nine distinct in/out selections.
- The environments progress through motion, direct expression, formal movement, travel, laptop work, arrival, office intention, calm reflection and a real business call.
- Café-laptop and phone-call footage appear once each and perform separate narrative jobs.
- The downloaded reference establishes a reusable design grammar: Didot/Bodoni contrast, giant hero words, asymmetry, color semantics, scale/slide/rotation and deliberate no-text air.
- The final “money” beat is earned through business activity, not watches, cars or cash.

## What this version tests

- Whether source-level uniqueness is enough to make a short conceptual story feel fresh from beginning to end.
- Whether editorial typography can add emotion and pattern interruption without becoming a conventional headline or obscuring Mansoor's identity.
- Whether a wider range of locations helps the abstract action → state → routine → performance arc read immediately.

## Footage still missing

- More current premium footage of Mansoor preparing alone before a consequential business moment.
- Current morning, fitness and planning routines where Mansoor is clearly visible without other people.
- Strong present-day transitions from calm preparation into an actual call, stage, meeting or close.
