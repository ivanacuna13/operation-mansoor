# Gold 02 v3 learning log

## What broke in v2

- Shot duration followed edit convenience instead of the spoken clause, so the image kept talking after the sentence had moved on.
- Two selected shots were not Mansoor. The catalog's identity label was treated as truth instead of a search hint.
- Old and current Mansoor footage appeared without a deliberate transformation arc.
- Captions were too low, too generic and paraphrased the audio instead of reproducing it exactly.
- The raw audio contained a false start that read as a background-audio hiccup.

## What v3 proves

- The audio can function as a screenplay when cuts are snapped to word-level clause boundaries.
- Current-era-only continuity is cleaner for a conceptual lesson than forcing a hardship-to-payoff timeline into every sound.
- Identity must be visually verified on every chosen in/out range, even when library documentation says `face? yes`.
- Semantic color is useful when it clarifies contrast: negative, process and desired state each get one consistent treatment.
- “Make money” is stronger on active business work than on a luxury receipt for this older Lone Wolf audience.

## Expanded-library result

- Selection expanded from the 28-file local favorites cache into the 602-item Drive catalog.
- The deeper catalog produced current phone, direct-to-camera, ocean and café-work footage that resolved the continuity problem.
- Catalog rows can still be wrong; one file described as Mansoor at a flip-chart was visibly another person and was rejected.

## Footage still missing

- More current premium footage of Mansoor preparing alone before a consequential business moment.
- Current morning, fitness and planning routines where Mansoor is clearly visible without other people.
- Strong present-day transitions from calm preparation into an actual call, stage, meeting or close.
