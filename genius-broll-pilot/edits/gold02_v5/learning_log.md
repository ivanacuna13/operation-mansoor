# Gold 02 v5 learning log

## What broke in v4

- The editorial typography was too transparent against changing footage and therefore failed the first job of captions: immediate readability.
- Moving compositions and multiple placements made the text system feel inconsistent.
- One flattened graphics clip in the XML prevented direct word-level timing changes in Premiere.

## What v5 changes

- The typography is now one repeatable system: two fixed lines, at most three words per line and left-to-right spoken-word reveals.
- Arial Black supplies the additional weight needed for an inverse treatment.
- The fill is the actual negative of the underlying picture—not a translucent red, yellow or white approximation.
- Every word exists as its own editable timeline clip while six tracks keep the Premiere timeline manageable.
- The approved no-repeat B-roll pacing and source selection remain unchanged.

## What this version tests

- Whether a fixed typographic location improves comprehension without distracting from the B-roll story.
- Whether footage-negative glyphs stay legible across bright, dark, skin-tone and sky backgrounds.
- Whether individual word clips provide the editability expected from the Premiere XML handoff.
