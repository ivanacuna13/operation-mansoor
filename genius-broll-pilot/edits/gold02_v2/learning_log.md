# Gold 02 v2 learning log

## What the failed v1 taught

- Leaving the original podcast footage underneath the edit changes the concept into a talking-head clip with occasional overlays. That is not Genius B-roll.
- A headline competes with the sound instead of letting the sound and picture become the hook.
- Sentence-length captions make the piece feel like a subtitled source clip rather than a short visual film.
- Attractive footage is not enough; every image must communicate the current spoken beat.

## What v2 changes

- The winning sound is treated as the screenplay and the source picture is removed completely.
- The visual story is continuous and causal: feeling → contrast → choice → routine → execution → control.
- Mansoor is present throughout so the story cannot drift into generic motivation.
- The first four seconds contain motion, face and emotion; the four-second cut creates a clear pattern interruption.
- Captions are reduced to rhythmic fragments and the headline is gone.

## Footage gap exposed

- The library still needs more current, premium footage of Mansoor visibly working alone, preparing before a consequential moment, leaving work on his own terms and moving through an ordinary calm family/provider day.
- Those receipts would communicate the older ICP's peace and control more directly than travel footage while reducing reuse of the Malibu and café clips.
