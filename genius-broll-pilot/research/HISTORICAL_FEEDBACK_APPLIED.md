Source: read-only search of existing messages and threads in MVP Agency `#content-team`. No review request was or should be sent there.

## Repeated rules found

- Bigger visual contrast creates more emotion. Complete the story; do not stop with Mansoor as the hero. End on what the viewer can become.
- Literal congruence is mandatory. A hardship line cannot show a high-end location; virtual work cannot show an in-person event; named people/claims must match the footage.
- Preserve the winning audio and plot. Iterations may change text, duration, size, speed or a minority of footage, but should not casually replace the validated emotional engine.
- For genuinely new/remade footage packages, 20% familiar / 80% different is the stated preference; avoid remix-looking repetition when the library has alternatives.
- Text must remain outside Mansoor's head/face.
- Avoid back-to-back quality drops.
- Limit traditional Arabic-dress footage when the intended distribution is the US.
- Avoid footage that could imply alcohol-related behavior.
- Never use unidentified watches or possessions as if they are Mansoor's.
