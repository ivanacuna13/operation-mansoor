# Skill — headline writing

## Use when

Creating the first-four-seconds visual hook for an engineered clip.

## Method

1. Define the clip's one-sentence value proposition.
2. Quote the spoken hook.
3. List source-supported context the spoken hook has not yet stated.
4. Generate at least ten options across distinct angles: outcome, mistake, contrast, mechanism, identity, stakes, confession, proof, contrarian claim, and curiosity.
5. Remove any option that repeats the spoken hook rather than adding to it.
6. Remove unsupported proof or vague clickbait.
7. Mock intended line breaks; maximum two lines.
8. Score, rank, and submit for approval.

## Score out of 100

- Adds to spoken hook: 25
- Promise/curiosity strength: 20
- Truth and evidence: 20
- Clarity at a glance: 15
- Specificity: 10
- Visual fit in two lines: 10

## Output fields

`option_id, exact_text, angle, adds_what, evidence, planned_line_break, score, risk, status`

The approved text becomes the filesystem-safe export filename. Preserve exact wording and capitalization in the visible headline.
