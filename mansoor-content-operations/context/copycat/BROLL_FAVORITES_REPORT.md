# Mansoor B-roll favorites and intelligence report

Updated: 2026-09-09

## Audit scope and confidence

This audit covers all 602 cataloged files in `BROLL_LIBRARY.md`: 601 files with a reviewed frame and one unreadable file. It compares the library against:

- the 100 published reels at or above 5K views in `Mansoor's Winners`;
- the six 100K+ winner analyses;
- `REVIEW_LOG.md`, including Ivan's stellar approval and revision notes;
- `STELLAR.md`, `MISTAKES.md`, `QUALITY.md`, `TRANSLATION.md`, and the wife-face lock/audit;
- the live `Create genius broll clip skill` task.

Important limitation: Instagram does not expose the Google Drive source file ID used inside every published reel. Therefore this report uses two evidence levels:

1. **Direct production evidence** — an asset/name or shot type was explicitly approved, revised, killed, or QC'd in the review log.
2. **Winner-mechanism alignment** — the cataloged asset directly supplies a causal visual role found in a 100K+ winner: origin receipt, family beneficiary, legacy, work proof, transformation, or freedom payoff.

Do not interpret the ranking as per-file view attribution when the source ID cannot be matched to a published reel.

## Scoring model

Each favorite is judged on five dimensions:

- **Performance evidence:** direct approval/revision evidence or match to a 100K+ mechanism.
- **Narrative information:** the shot proves something specific instead of merely looking expensive.
- **Mansoor identity:** Mansoor is recognizable and the footage could not belong to any generic creator.
- **Emotional ICP fit:** family, freedom, legacy, sacrifice, leadership, faith-safe identity, or transformation.
- **Reusability:** the shot can support multiple audio beats without becoming misleading or repetitive.

Risk deductions apply for visible wife face, wrong-person ambiguity, faceless opening, youth-flex energy, weapons, cash/watch-only imagery, smoking, frozen-frame dependence, or near-duplicate overuse.

## Directly learned from approvals and failures

### Relationship correction — 2026-09-15

The catalog previously mislabeled footage of Mansoor with a young girl as his daughter. Mansoor does not have a daughter. Those assets are prohibited until the person's identity, relationship, privacy status, and intended story use are explicitly verified. Never infer family relationships from appearance or folder placement.

### What worked

- `Dad: “Stick to the safe path kid.” / My crazy dream:` is the explicit stellar bar.
- Its winning visual grammar was **Mansoor face first → black-and-white quote/work chapter → color drop → travel/car life**.
- `IMG_5004.JPG` became the required homeless opener for `you stuck with it`.
- `face_cafe.MOV` replaced a hallway because the shot needed to prove Mansoor was working.
- `IMG_9573.MOV` supplied the car/lifestyle ending after the origin chapter.
- Short face-first moments such as the Prius, jet cabin, happy driver, mountain selfie, and grey-sweatshirt gesture repeatedly passed later QC gates.

### What failed

- Generic people in a hallway did not communicate work.
- A family hug did not satisfy a requested lifestyle payoff.
- Frozen-frame padding, weak ripped audio, and long text killed an otherwise plausible idea.
- The same/near-identical clip repeated within five seconds reads as a glitch.
- Visible wife face is always disallowed.
- Cash, watches, cigars, and cars without a relational or transformation context skew toward shallow flex content. They are accents, not anchors.

## What the numbers teach about the library

The library is numerically deepest in travel (81), face (80), formal (58), and car (49). It is much thinner in family (22) and office/work (28). Yet family, legacy, vulnerability, and real work explain the strongest winner mechanisms.

That creates a strategic imbalance:

- The library has abundant **payoff footage**.
- It has fewer **cause, sacrifice, relationship, and work receipts**.
- Editors will default to cars/travel because they are easy to find unless the system explicitly prioritizes narrative function.

The next shoot should not primarily add another exotic-car montage. It should add high-quality, contemporary footage of:

1. Mansoor working alone before others arrive.
2. Mansoor coaching one agent and then a room.
3. Family moments with Dad, grandfather/elders, siblings, and children.
4. Quiet provider moments: calling family, paying for an experience, arriving together, handing over a meaningful gift.
5. Present-day reenactment-neutral receipts of the Prius/office chapter without pretending old footage is new evidence.
6. Faith/routine/community moments that are natural and non-performative.
7. Calm freedom: weekday morning, travel with family, phone off, leaving the office, ordinary peace.

## Recommended library changes

Turn the current find-first catalog into a shot-intelligence layer. Each asset should gain:

- `favorite_tier`: S / A / utility / avoid
- `story_role`: anchor / cause / struggle / work / bridge / proof / beneficiary / payoff / transition
- `people`: Mansoor / Dad / grandfather / child / wife-safe / others
- `emotion`: pressure / hope / gratitude / joy / freedom / authority / intimacy
- `era`: origin / transition / present
- `face_first_safe`: yes/no
- `wife_face_safe_range`: exact in/out or prohibited
- `motion_quality`: strong / usable / still / blurred / unreadable
- `usage_count`, `last_used`, and `recent_render_ids`
- `winner_family`: family payoff / legacy / origin transformation / work-to-freedom / authority
- shot-level in/out ranges, because one file can contain both safe and prohibited frames

Favorites should be logical metadata or Drive shortcuts, not duplicate media files.

## Guardrails for Genius B-roll

- Mansoor must appear in the first one to three seconds and recur often enough that the video remains his.
- Every B-roll change must serve a spoken beat: literal evidence, emotional interpretation, credibility, bridge, or payoff.
- Do not cover strong facial/vocal emotion with generic luxury footage.
- Use a contrast arc; do not begin and end at the same emotional/status level.
- Outside-creator audio needs an explicit rights/attribution decision before publishing.
- Winning source audio validates the source post, not automatically Mansoor ICP fit. Score both separately.
- Preserve exact source transcript, edit transcript, removals, and timing so the audio is never misleadingly reconstructed.
- Log every source file ID and in/out range in the render manifest.
- Enforce the existing wife-face, wrong-Dad, freeze, text, and audio gates.

## Adjacent workflows worth building

1. **B-roll gap detector** — compares approved scripts/audio beat maps against library roles and automatically creates a prioritized shoot list.
2. **Winner lineage tracker** — connects source winner → audio → headline → B-roll manifest → render → post → 24h/72h/7d performance.
3. **Favorite/fatigue manager** — recommends S/A assets but blocks recent overuse and near-duplicate shots.
4. **Origin-story assembler** — specialized workflow for Prius/homeless/early-office → current-life transformations.
5. **Family legacy assembler** — father, grandfather, sibling, children, and face-safe relationship templates.
6. **Authority-to-freedom assembler** — teaching/podium/team proof followed by calm time/travel payoff for the older ICP.
7. **Trial-reel laboratory** — produces controlled hook/first-frame variants, tracks each trial separately, and promotes only verified winners.
8. **Audio intelligence library** — transcripts, emotional beats, rights status, source metrics, speaker, reuse count, and target ICP for Mansoor and external audio.
9. **Review-learning compiler** — turns Slack/Frame approval, revision, and rejection reasons into testable future gates.
10. **Shoot-day coverage planner** — requests complete visual sequences instead of isolated clips: wide, medium, close, action, reaction, transition, and payoff.
