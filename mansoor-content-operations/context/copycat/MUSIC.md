# Music lock (LOCKED 2026-08-16)

No bad profanity in lyrics playing on a delivered remake. Distasteful lyrics too (slurs, explicit sex, ugly drug-dealer talk). If you would not play it in front of mom, it does not ship with vocals.

## Scout

Listen when you watch. Profane music does **not** kill the idea.

If the reel passes the other gates, file it. Mark the card:

`MUSIC: profane — Cutter must replace with instrumental`

If the lyrics are clean:

`MUSIC: clean`

Scout can approve / file a reel with profane music. The editor replaces the track. Do not skip a winner because the song is dirty.

## Cutter

Hard fail: delivering a cut with profane or distasteful lyrics playing.

If Scout marked `MUSIC: profane`, or you hear it yourself:

1. Find an instrumental of that song (official instrumental, no-vocals, or a clean beat with the same energy and drop).
2. Cut it to the remake duration. Same length as the original audio / visual cut.
3. Use that. Never the dirty vocal.

If you cannot find an instrumental, stop and tell Eddie. Do not ship the dirty vocal. Do not invent a random different-vibe track unless it still carries the same drop.

Write the replacement track name in the cut note.

## What "distasteful" means

Swear words on the vocal. Slurs. Explicit sex. Anything you would mute in a room with family. Energy and a dirty beat are fine. Words on the vocal are the problem.
