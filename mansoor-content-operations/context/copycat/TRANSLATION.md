# Translation layer

Read this before you write a single Ideas for Review card. If the card does not include this layer, it is not ready to edit.

This is not a noun swap. "Internship" to "9-5" is an example of the layer, not the layer.

---

## Two worlds

Every original reel has a **framework** and a **world**.

The **framework** is what we steal: where the text sits, when it dies, the cut rhythm, the song, the joke shape. Sarcasm aimed at the person who just called the path fake. A hold on one line while the life plays. Plan A / Plan B. That structure.

The **world** is the original creator's. Their trophies, their insults, their idea of a good life. Unpaid internships. Two Lambos at 24. "I did it all myself." We do not steal that world.

We put **Mansoor's intended audience** in the same framework.

---

## Intended audience (ICP)

Locked from Mansoor's interview (458 turns, 2026-07-19). Not vibes. Not "a hustler." This specific person:

18 to 28. US only. Ambitious. Usually a college dropout. Athletics often in the background. A lot of them come from a single-parent house. They hit adversity early. They need to make money or prove something to their parents.

They open Instagram, see everyone in a Lambo living the dream, and feel left behind. They want to catch up. They do not want more college. They do not want a nine-to-five for somebody else. They want to travel. They want to support their family and not live on anyone else's terms. They say they want more money and their first Lambo. Underneath that they feel behind.

They think the problem is they do not have a system. Mansoor says the real problem is identity. Insurance is the vehicle if they are in the United States. They look at him as Unc. They send a reel to their boy or their mom.

This machine is the US recruit path. Saudi / UAE virality is a different offer.

**Not the intended audience:** a business owner who wants to "scale," someone outside the US who cannot get licensed, final-expense, a 40-year-old with a book, a gym-only or dating-only page with no money-or-family wound, anyone who needs a 6-step close on the reel.

## Their values (plural)

Write from these. Not one "value." Not a menu you pick from at random.

- Freedom from the nine-to-five. The safe job is the cage they are running away from, not a fallback.
- No more college as the respectable path.
- Family: mom, the people they have to prove something to, later a wife and kids. Support them. Do not live on anyone else's terms.
- God, and the people they love. Not lone-wolf guru. Not "I did it all myself" as the whole story.
- Their boy in it with them.
- Travel. Freedom to go.
- A life that looks like they made it. A Lambo is allowed. A Lambo as the entire personality is how this becomes a cringey influencer page, which it is not.

Insurance looks small or fake until the life proves it isn't. "He sells insurance" is an insult they already hear.

## What translation actually is

Name the framework. Then rewrite the line and pick the pictures so they prove the intended audience's values, not the original creator's world.

If you only swap a noun and keep the original meaning, you missed it.
If you write a generic clapback with no framework, you missed it.
If the new line and the shots do not agree, you missed it.

### Example: "Sure bro, everything's a scam go cop that unpaid internship"

The framework is not "internship." It is sarcasm aimed at the person who just called this path fake. The original tells that person to go back to the life *they* understand: unpaid internship, the respectable starter lane.

For the intended audience, the life they are running away from is the safe, secure nine-to-five (and the degree that feeds it). "Insurance is a scam" is the insult they already hear.

So the remake keeps the sarcastic shove. The thing you shove them back into is the 9-5, not an internship this person may not even have.

Wrong: a generic "haters gonna hate" on a car.
Wrong: keep "unpaid internship" because that was the original joke.
Right: same shove, aimed at the cage they actually left.

### Example: "it hits different when you did it all yourself"

The framework is one line over a hold. The original world is lone-wolf, self-made.

The intended audience's values include people they love, God, family, their boy. The remake can still be a hold on one line. The line should not stay "all yourself" unless you chose that on purpose.

A convertible and God in the same reel is fine when you chose it: the car is the night, the line is who they did it with. It is wrong when the line says family and every shot is an empty car lot. That is a sticker on someone else's video.

### Example: "they told me I couldn't do it / 2 lambos at 24"

The framework is their words first, then the life as the rebuttal. Lambos are not banned. Lambos as the only proof is how it gets cringe.

For this audience the rebuttal can be wife, a loving family, freedom to travel, the night they actually wanted. If the text is about family, show family, friends, wife, mom. If the text is about walking away from a 9-5, the night, the trip, the table with their people, a car they earned, those can all be true in one cut when each shot is there on purpose.

## Pictures follow the line on purpose

The text decides the slots. Not the other way around.

| If the line is about | The shots should include |
|---|---|
| Family / mom / prove them wrong | Family, mom, wife-without-her-face (ring/Chanel/hands/from behind), kids, the table, the house |
| People you love / God | Those people, the quiet proof, not a solo flex with a sticker |
| Their boy | Two faces, the pact, not a lone hero |
| Running from a 9-5 | Leaving that life, the night after, travel, the life they chose |
| They laughed at insurance | The insult first, then a life that is bigger than the job title |
| Made it | Allowed: cars, travel, nights. Not allowed: car lot as the whole video when the line was about family |

Mansoor has the library: cars, family, planes, vacations, work, night. Use the slot the line requires. Do not default to Lambos because the original did.

## The card is not ready until this is written

Every Ideas for Review Notes block must include all of this. Missing one means it is not ready to edit.

```
HOW TO CUT THIS
- Framework (what we steal): the joke shape / hold / text placement / cut / song
- Original world: their insult, their trophy, their idea of a good life
- Intended audience values this remake proves: (name them from the list above)
- New line: exact on-screen text
- Shots that prove that line: which library slots, and why those slots match the line
- First 3 seconds: picture, text, placement
- Then: the cut this one actually does
- Song: energy + drop. MUSIC: clean | profane — Cutter must replace with instrumental
- Layer + who they send it to (boy / mom)
- Do not: talking-head, offer, DM, lone-wolf sticker, Lambo-only when the line was about family
- Do not: wife-face. Close-seconds only (ring, Chanel bag, holding hands, hands/bag/dress, couple from behind).
```

Line and shots must agree. If they do not, do not file.
