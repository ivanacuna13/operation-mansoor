# 00 - MASTER CONTEXT INDEX

## WHAT THIS IS

This is the context spine for Mansoor Alzayer (Mansoor), a life insurance agency owner recruiting agents. These documents are the foundational context for every future deliverable: short-form scripts, YouTube concepts, lead magnets, funnel pages, email sequences, and sales call scripts.

Feed any of these documents into any AI system alongside a task prompt, and the AI should be able to produce on-brand, on-strategy content without needing additional background.

## HOW TO USE

1. For any content task, load the relevant documents from this spine into the AI's context.
2. At minimum, always load: 01 Client Profile, 02 ICP Profiles, 04 Messaging and Verbiage Rules, and 08 Voice and Tone Profile.
3. Add the specific document relevant to the task (e.g., 06 Content Pillars for script writing, 07 Lead Magnets for funnel building).
4. Write the task prompt referencing the specific ICP, pillar, and deliverable type.

## THE ONE RULE THAT OVERRIDES EVERYTHING

Never use the words "recruiting," "building," "producer," or "builder" when addressing the audience. These words alienate half the industry instantly. Use "scaling," "duplication," "leverage," "client acquisition," and "revenue-driven activities" instead. This is non-negotiable and applies to every deliverable.
