# 07 - LEAD MAGNETS AND FUNNELS

## LEAD MAGNET PRINCIPLES (FROM THE CLIENT'S ANALYSIS)

The client reverse-engineered why a lead magnet he recently consumed was effective. Three factors made it work:

1. It solved a big problem he cared enough about to solve. (In that case, Instagram optimization to his ICP.)
2. It delivered value very fast. Under five minutes. Quick payoff.
3. It gave direct, customized pointers. It scanned his page, told him what was good, told him what was bad, and felt custom-tailored rather than cookie-cutter.

The natural progression: after consuming the lead magnet, he felt lost and realized he needed a guide. That feeling naturally led to booking a call.

Every lead magnet must deliver all three: solve a real problem, deliver fast, feel customized, and create a natural bridge to "I need help with this."

## LEAD MAGNET 1: THE PASSIVE INCOME CALCULATOR (PRIMARY)

### Concept
An interactive tool where the agent enters their current monthly production and team size. The tool calculates their path to passive income through team building and shows the gap between their current trajectory and the leveraged trajectory.

### How It Works
- Landing page with a simple form.
- Fields: current monthly production, current team size, state licensed in, years in insurance.
- On submit, a loading screen with a progress bar appears.
- Results page shows two line graphs: one linear (their current trajectory) and one exponential (the leveraged trajectory).
- The answer type is the same for everyone: current path is linear, leveraged path is exponential. The exact numbers differ based on inputs, but the shape is always the same.
- Below the graphs: "Here is what it will take you on your current path: [X agents, Y years]. Here is what it takes on the leveraged path: [10% of that]."
- CTA: "Want to see how the leveraged path actually works? Book a call."

### Why It Works
- Solves a big problem: "Can I actually get to $100K/month, and what will it take?"
- Delivers fast: under five minutes.
- Feels customized: uses their actual numbers.
- Creates the realization: "I do not need 300 agents. I need 60 deployed correctly."
- Natural bridge to a call: the calculator shows the gap, the call fills the gap.

### The Narrative Hook (For The Video That Drives To It)
"I discovered a method that lets you get to $100K/month with a fraction of the team you think you need. DM me the word [LEVERAGE] and I'll send you the calculator that shows you the difference between your current path and the leveraged path."

## LEAD MAGNET 2: THE DIAGNOSTIC (SELF-SELECTING FILTER)

### Concept
A short diagnostic that asks the agent to self-identify as either sales-heavy or recruiting-heavy. This splits leads into the two ICP tracks and determines which content and which call angle to serve them.

### How It Works
- 1-2 questions.
- Question 1: "Which of these is your bigger bottleneck right now?" Options: "I can sell but I cannot scale beyond myself" (ICP 2) or "I have a team but they cannot sell" (ICP 1).
- Based on the answer, the lead is tagged and routed to the corresponding content track and call angle.
- Result page: "You are a [Lone Wolf / Builder]. Here is your biggest bottleneck. Here is the framework that fixes it. Want to see how? Book a call."

### Why It Works
- Self-diagnosis builds credibility. The prospect feels understood.
- Filters leads before the call. The closer knows which angle to take.
- Splits the content. 15 videos target one diagnosis, 15 target the other.
- Creates commitment. The prospect has publicly identified their problem.

## LEAD MAGNET 3: THE $150K/MONTH BLUEPRINT (CONTENT-DRIVEN)

### Concept
A short document or video that lays out the 90-day plan to a $150K month (write $25K, teach 5 people to write $25K). This is the offer framework packaged as a lead magnet.

### How It Works
- Driven from YouTube long-form videos and short-form videos that reference the blueprint.
- The blueprint lays out the two steps, the bottlenecks, the beliefs, and the 90-day process.
- CTA: "Want me to map this to your specific numbers? Book a call."

---

## LEAD MAGNET 4: THE CHARGEBACK AND PERSISTENCY AUDIT (ICP 2 SPECIFIC)

### Concept
A tool or checklist that helps the lone wolf producer assess the quality of their book of business. Are they submitting bad business? What is their chargeback rate? What is their persistency?

### Why It Works
- Speaks directly to ICP 2's deepest fear (chargebacks, roll-up debt, business falling off the books).
- Positions the client as someone who cares about quality, not just volume.
- Natural bridge: "Your persistency is below where it should be. Here is why, and here is how we fix it. Book a call."

## FUNNEL ARCHITECTURE

### Top of Funnel (Attention)
- Live content (Instagram, YouTube).
- Sales-focused content (broad, US-filtered).
- YouTube long-form (framework-driven, story-led).
- Organic Instagram posts (5 per day, comment-based CTAs).

### Middle of Funnel (Capture)
- Scripted short-form videos (15 per ICP).
- Each video has a DM CTA: "DM me the word [X] and I'll send you [Y]."
- DM triggers delivery of the lead magnet.

### Bottom of Funnel (Convert)
- Lead magnet delivery (calculator, diagnostic, blueprint).
- Lead magnet creates the realization and the feeling of "I need a guide."
- Booking page (Calendly or similar, embedded on the lead magnet result page).
- Confirmation page with a due-diligence video or trust assets.

### Post-Booking (Show and Close)
- Pre-call email/SMS sequence (to be designed).
- The call: diagnose which stage they are on, show them the next rung, map the framework to their numbers, present the offer.

---

## THE DM CTA STRUCTURE

Every short-form video ends with a DM CTA. The structure:

1. Hook (first 3 seconds): call out the specific pain or situation.
2. Story/framework: deliver the realization or the framework name.
3. CTA: "DM me the word [WORD] and I'll send you [THING]."

Examples:
- "DM me LEVERAGE and I'll send you the calculator that shows you the difference between your current path and the leveraged path."
- "DM me DUPLICATE and I'll send you the framework for creating carbon copies of yourself."
- "DM me DIAGNOSE and I'll send you the 60-second assessment that tells you your biggest bottleneck."

---

## THE BOOKING PAGE RULE

The booking page should be embedded on the lead magnet result page, not a separate step. Reduce friction. The prospect finishes the calculator, sees the gap, and books right there. No separate scheduler page, no extra click.

The confirmation page should include:
- A short video framing what the call will cover.
- Trust assets (testimonials, case studies, the Pinnacle benchmark).
- A due-diligence element if there is negative search content to address.
