## PHASE 1.5: MARCZELL CONCEPT TRANSLATION MATRIX

### What This Is
Mansoor has course transcripts from Marczell's high-performance coaching program. He wants to weave these concepts into his insurance content through the two ICP lenses. Document 11 (Marczell Integration Strategy) defines the rules for how to do this without overdiluting the content.

### The Process
1. Upload the Marczell transcripts.
2. Extract every distinct concept, framework, and mental model.
3. Translate each into an insurance-specific container (renamed framework).
4. Apply each to one ICP per piece of content (never both in one video).
5. Compile into the Marczell Concept Translation Matrix (Document 12).
6. Use the matrix as source material for 30-40% of the short-form scripts.

### The Anti-Dilution Rules (Summary)
- One Marczell concept per video.
- The concept is never named as Marczell's. It gets a new container name.
- The insurance pain opens the video. The concept is the mechanism, not the hook.
- Every concept must be grounded in a concrete insurance example.
- Limit to 2-3 Marczell-originated terms per video.
- Only 30-40% of scripts should use a Marczell-derived concept. The rest are pure insurance strategy.

### Input Needed From Client
- The Marczell course transcripts (upload when ready).
- Confirmation of which concepts resonate most with him personally.
- Any concepts he has already started using or naming.

## PHASE 2: SHORT-FORM SCRIPT BATCH (30 VIDEOS)

### Structure
30 fully scripted short-form videos, written for teleprompter line-by-line recording (Calloway method).

### Split
- 15 videos targeting ICP 1 (The Recruiter: has a team, cannot sell, stuck at $30K/month).
- 15 videos targeting ICP 2 (The Lone Wolf: can sell, no team, stuck at $25K/month).

### Per-Video Structure
Each script follows this arc:
1. Hook (first 3 seconds): call out the specific pain or situation for that ICP.
2. Story or framework: deliver the realization or introduce a named framework.
3. The shift: the mind-shift the viewer needs (from recognition to income, from lone wolf to carbon copies, etc).
4. CTA: DM me the word [WORD] and I'll send you [LEAD MAGNET].

### Pillar Distribution (Per ICP)
Of the 15 videos per ICP:
- 5 identity videos (shift how they see themselves).
- 5 skill-set videos (teach the missing half).
- 5 strategy videos (lay out the path, the stage ladder, the 90-day plan).

### Script Format
Written as discrete deliverable lines for teleprompter. Short lines, one breath each. Flow naturally when read sequentially. No bullet points, no lists in the spoken text.

### Input Needed From Client
- Redline on any language he is uncomfortable saying.
- Confirmation of the DM CTA words and which lead magnet each maps to.
- Outfit and environment plan for the filming batch.

## SEQUENCING RULE

Do not skip ahead. The context spine feeds the scripts. The scripts feed the lead magnets (via DM CTAs). The lead magnets feed the funnel. The funnel feeds the call. Each phase depends on the one before it. If the context spine is wrong, everything downstream is wrong. That is why these documents exist first.
