# 02 - ICP PROFILES

There are two distinct ICPs. Both are stuck at roughly $30K/month but for opposite reasons. The content must be split: 15 short-form videos targeting ICP 1, 15 targeting ICP 2. Never blend them in a single video.

---

## ICP 1: THE RECRUITER (Recruiting-Heavy, Sales-Weak)

### Origin Culture
Comes from PHP, Primerica, WFG. These are network marketing / warm-market model companies. In these companies, recruiting is the revenue driver. They do not spend money on leads. They pitch friends, family, and warm contacts. The phrase "do you keep your business options open?" is a trauma trigger for anyone who has been through it.

### Current State
Makes around $30K/month. Has a team, sometimes a large team. Knows how to recruit. Sucks at sales. Never learned how to sell insurance professionally because the model never required it. Does not buy leads. Does not talk about profitability because there is no lead spend to debate.

### Why They Are Stuck
They have a massive team but are broke. One leg is doing most of the volume. They chase recognition and flash production numbers, but production gives no indication of what is actually in the bank account. They associate making $100K/month with climbing the MLM ladder (MD to EMD to VP) and having a massive chip. They do not know there is another way.

### The Realization They Need
They do not need 300 agents. They need 60, deployed in a different vehicle, with sales training installed. The same team they already have could be making $100K/month if they instilled sales training and had better contracts. They are closer than they think.

### Mind Shift To Deliver
You already have the team. You already know how to recruit. What you do not have is sales training and a compensation model that rewards production quality, not headcount. Stop chasing recognition. Start chasing take-home income.

### Verbiage That Resonates
Scaling, duplication, leverage, deploying a sales force in a different vehicle, passive income, take-home income vs production.

### Verbiage That Repels
Recruiting, building, warm market, "do you keep your business options open," producer, mindset, motivation.

---

## ICP 2: THE LONE WOLF PRODUCER (Sales-Heavy, Recruiting-Weak)

### Origin Culture
Comes from FFL, Symmetry, and similar lead-buying model agencies. These are sales cultures. They sell a lot. They buy leads. They talk about cost per lead, profitability, persistency, chargebacks. They never talk about recruiting. In FFL specifically, there is no revenue driver for recruiting, so it is never discussed.

### Current State
Does $25K/month in production personally. Working 80 hours a week. Everything rides on them. They are the only producer. They spend $2K/week on leads. They have mastered the script, the presentation, objection handling. They are a professional salesperson with routines and KPIs. But if they stop, everything stops.

### Why They Are Stuck
They have never bought into the idea of scaling. They view recruiting as the thing the MLM guys do, the thing where you hit up friends and family. They are already doing the hardest part (selling) but they are doing it alone, one-on-one, with no audience and no duplication. They are leaving massive money on the table every month because they are not selling in front of an audience and not creating carbon copies of themselves.

### The Realization They Need
You are already doing it. The fact that you sell $25K/month means you are the prime example. You just have not created carbon copies of yourself. You are costing yourself money every month by not having 10, 15, 30, 50 people watch you do it. Stop selling insurance for the commission. The commission is the cherry on top. Sell insurance for the duplication.

### Mind Shift To Deliver
Think like a billionaire, not like you are cute for making $100K-$300K a year selling insurance. You are not cute if you make nothing passively. Whatever you are doing right now stops the moment you stop. You are already the machine. Now build the factory around the machine.

### Verbiage That Resonates
Duplication, carbon copies, leverage, client acquisition, revenue-driven activities, scaling, passive income, selling in front of an audience.

### Verbiage That Repels
Recruiting, building, warm market, producer, builder, mindset, motivation, flashy lifestyle.

---

## SHARED CHARACTERISTICS (BOTH ICPs)

### Financial Frame
Both can wrap their heads around $100K/month in income. Neither can wrap their heads around $1M/month. Use $100K/month as the hook, not $1M/month. The closer something is, the more motivating it is (Marcel's proximity principle). Millionaire in a title outperforms billionaire because people cannot conceive of a billion. Same logic applies here.

### Income vs Production
Never talk about production numbers as the goal. Production is a vanity metric in this market. The line that lands: "Who cares about production? How much did you take home?" Always frame the target as income, not production.

### Geographic Filter
Both must be US-based life insurance agents. The content should reference US-specific concepts (national debt, 401k, US insurance carriers, US lead markets) so the algorithm naturally filters out irrelevant international reach.

### What They Do Not Want
Motivation, mindset content, flashy lifestyle, twerking, rented cars, generic biz-op framing. They want selling systems, recruiting systems, leadership development. They are operators, not dreamers.

### The Bridge Concept
Recruiting people need sales but hate sales. Sales people need recruiting but hate recruiting. The content must wrap the medicine in candy. Take recruiting and put it in the container of leverage or strategy. Take sales and lead-buying and put it in the container of client acquisition and revenue-driven activities. This is the Marcel cigarette-into-raw-chicken-container technique applied to insurance concepts.
