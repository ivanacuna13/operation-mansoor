# 05 - OFFER AND POSITIONING

## CORE OFFER STATEMENT

Help life insurance agents duplicate, build a team, and scale. Two segments, two escalator paths.

### Segment A: The $30K/month Producer or Recruiter
Target: scale to $100K/month with passive income.
Mechanism: install the missing half of their skill set (sales training for recruiters, duplication systems for salespeople) and restructure their team deployment.

### Segment B: The $100K/month Sales Leader
Target: scale to $300K/month with systems and leverage.
Prerequisite: already has a team of 5 to 10.
Mechanism: teach them to develop leaders, not just producers. Go wide personally, go deep with the top 3, build three layers deep.

## THE STAGE LADDER (FROM MANSOOR NOTES)

This is the progression framework. Every prospect sits on one of these rungs. The content and the call must diagnose which rung they are on and show them the next one.

| Stage | Team Size | Bottleneck | Skill Required | Proof | Business Revenue | Income |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Lone Wolf | 0 | Has not learned recruiting | Master sales | Writing $25K in production | $25K/mo | $8-10K/mo |
| The Builder | 5 | Only trained sales, has not trained recruiting | Master sales and recruiting | Write $25K, taught 5 others to write $25K | $150K/mo | $20K/mo |
| The Leader | 25 | Taught guys to sell and recruit, but did not develop leaders | Master sales, recruiting, and sales training | Write $25K, taught 5 others to teach 5 others to write $25K | $650K/mo | $75K/mo |
| The Leader of Leaders | 125 | Needs leadership development mastery | Master sales, recruiting, sales training, and leadership development | Taught 125 to write $25K | $3.125M/mo | $250K/mo |

### How To Use This In Content
Every video should implicitly or explicitly place the viewer on this ladder. The hook is: "You are here. Here is why you are stuck. Here is the next rung. Here is how to get there."

## THE 90 DAY PLAN TO A $150K MONTH

### Step 1: Write $25K (Selling Mastery)
1. Spend $2,000/week on leads. Cannot be cheap on your business and marketing. Full pipeline, invest in the business.
2. Master your script, presentations, and objections. Product knowledge, objection handling.
3. Become a professional salesperson. Routines, KPIs, system.

### Step 2: Teach 5 People How To Write $25K (Recruiting and Leadership Mastery)

#### Bottlenecks Preventing This
1. Do not know how to recruit.
2. Do not know the math and formula to get to passive income.
3. Do not know how to train and develop leaders.
4. Operate out of scarcity rather than systems.

#### Beliefs Blocking This
1. They would rather produce than build systems.
2. Selling is a money-making activity, training is not.

#### The 90 Day Process
1. Identify recruiting channels (warm market and social media).
2. Learn and use recruiting scripts (3 good qualities, etc).
3. Book recruiting appointments.
4. Get them licensed.
5. Teach how to write $25K by having them watch you write $25K.
6. Provide them leads.
7. Staff handles contracting and logistics. You only exist to teach sales and develop leaders.

### One Sentence Summary
You master selling but also recruiting and training others how to sell.
Outcome: $125K/month.

## THE PLAN TO A $650K MONTH

Teach 5 people to write $25K and teach 5 others to write $25K.

### Bottleneck
1. They have a team of producers, not builders.
2. They are the only one recruiting.
3. They have not developed leaders (people who can not only recruit but teach others to recruit).

### One Sentence Summary
You have not only mastered selling and recruiting, but mastered selling, recruiting, and developing leaders who have mastered sales, recruiting, and developing leaders.
Outcome: $600K+/month.

## POSITIONING ANGLE

### Against The MLM/Network Marketing Model (PHP, Primerica, WFG)
The model teaches you to chase recognition, climb a ladder (MD, EMD, VP), and build a team of 300 over 20 years. It does not teach sales. It does not teach profitability. It does not teach lead acquisition. The production numbers are vanity. The bank accounts are empty.

### Against The Pure Sales/Lead-Buying Model (FFL, Symmetry)
The model teaches you to sell and buy leads. It never teaches recruiting or duplication. You stay a lone wolf. Your income is 100% tied to your personal hours. If you stop, it stops. You are a great salesperson with a fragile business.

### The Combined Model (What The Client Offers)
Take the recruiting and building skill from the MLM world. Take the sales and lead-acquisition skill from the lead-buying world. Combine them. Wrap both in new language (leverage, duplication, client acquisition) so neither side rejects it. Deploy the combined sales force in a vehicle that compounds. Result: $100K/month with 60 agents instead of $100K/month with 300.

### The Pinnacle Benchmark
Pinnacle Financial Group (Joe Baszo, left FFL, came from solar, was a recruiter not a producer, partnered with people stronger than him in his weak areas) went from zero to over $10M/month in production in two years. The caliber of person they attract: clean-cut, professional, polished, swag. This is the model. This is the proof it works.

## THE DIAGNOSTIC HOOK (SELF-SELECTING LEAD MAGNET CONCEPT)

A diagnostic that asks the prospect to self-identify as either sales-heavy or recruiting-heavy. This filters leads into the two ICP tracks and allows the content to be split accordingly. If someone fills this out, you immediately know which of the two video sets to serve them and which angle to take on the sales call.

This also functions as a qualifier: it shows the prospect that you understand there are two distinct problems in the industry, which builds credibility instantly.
