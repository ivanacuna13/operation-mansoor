# 08 - VOICE AND TONE PROFILE

## THE CLIENT'S ON-CAMERA VOICE

The client is the on-camera talent. Scripts are written for him to read via teleprompter. The voice must sound like him, not like a generic marketer. Based on the transcripts, his natural voice has these characteristics:

### Conversational and Direct
He speaks in a conversational, almost stream-of-consciousness way. He uses "bro" frequently. He tells stories. He gets excited when a concept clicks. He is not polished or corporate. He is raw and authentic.

### Framework-Driven
When he lands on a framework or a mental model, his energy spikes. He gets animated. This is where he is strongest. The scripts should lean into this: name the framework, build the container, deliver the realization.

### Story-Led
He naturally tells stories to make points. The origin story (leaving PHP, seeing 60 guys out-earn 300) is his spine. He also tells stories about other operators (Vic Landor, Joe Baszo, Danny Klein, Chris Hart) to illustrate points. Scripts should include story beats, not just information dumps.

### Blunt About The Industry
He is blunt about what is broken in the insurance industry. He calls out production numbers as vanity. He calls out the MLM ladder as a trap. He calls out warm market tactics as trauma-inducing. The scripts should not soften this. The bluntness is the credibility.

### High-Performance Language (Imported)
He wants to import language from Marcel, Tony Robbins, and Wes Watson. Words like resonance, emotional noise, emotional state, timelines, identity. This language signals elite to the right audience and differentiates him from every other insurance recruiter. Use it sparingly and always grounded in a concrete insurance example so it does not float into generic self-help.

## TONE RULES FOR SCRIPTS

### Do
- Speak directly to the specific situation of the viewer (lone wolf or recruiter).
- Use the client's origin story as the narrative anchor.
- Name frameworks. Build containers. Create the "who is this guy and what is that?" intrigue.
- Be blunt about what is broken. Call out production vanity, the MLM ladder, warm market trauma.
- Frame every outcome as income, not production.
- Use $100K/month as the hook, not $1M/month.
- End with a DM CTA that delivers a specific lead magnet.
- Reference US-specific concepts to filter the audience.

### Do Not
- Use the words recruiting, building, producer, builder, mindset, motivation.
- Use flashy lifestyle framing.
- Use generic biz-op language.
- Celebrate production numbers.
- Use $1M/month as the primary hook.
- Reference international or generic concepts that would attract non-US audiences.
- Soften the bluntness. The bluntness is the brand.
- Use lists or bullet points in spoken scripts. Write in flowing, conversational prose.

## THE CALLAWAY RECORDING METHOD (PRODUCTION NOTE)

Short-form scripts are written for line-by-line recording. The client reads one line, looks at the camera, delivers it, looks down at the teleprompter, reads the next line, delivers it. Cuts are invisible because he is stationary. This means:

- Each line should be short enough to deliver in one breath.
- Lines should flow naturally when read sequentially.
- The script should be written as discrete deliverable lines, not as paragraphs.
- The editor cuts between lines. No need for continuous takes.

For YouTube long-form, the script is an outline, not a teleprompter read. The client delivers it more naturally, off the cuff, guided by the outline structure.

## OUTFIT AND ENVIRONMENT NOTES

The client films in batches with outfit changes to create volume. He has filmed in hotel rooms and plans to film in scenic locations (Bahamas referenced). The environment should signal professional but not flashy. Clean-cut, polished, swag. The Pinnacle Financial Group aesthetic. Not twerking, not rented cars, not generic influencer setups.

The filming environment also signals audience targeting. A podcast studio aesthetic attracts business-content consumers. Outdoor or travel aesthetics attract freedom-seeking entrepreneurs. Choose environments that match the ICP being targeted in that batch.
