# Mansoor context

## Known context from the training example

- Speaker: Mansoor.
- Typical source: high-resolution vertical talking-head footage capable of filling a 1080x1920 frame.
- Example source theme: routines, emotion, effective action, identity, business, and life improvement.
- Example business proof used by the editor: Mansoor made $3 million in one year after establishing the routine discussed. This claim is example-specific and must not be generalized to unrelated footage without source evidence.
- Teaching clips may work without music.

## Context discipline

Before selecting clips from a new source, write a source-specific context brief. It must state the whole recording's thesis, audience promise, speaker claims, examples, narrative arc, and any facts that may support headlines. Never reuse the $3M claim or other example context unless the new source or approved brand pack supports it.

## Naming

Use `Mansoor` in project artifacts unless the current job brief specifies another canonical spelling. The training transcript alternates spellings; this package standardizes on the user's spelling.
