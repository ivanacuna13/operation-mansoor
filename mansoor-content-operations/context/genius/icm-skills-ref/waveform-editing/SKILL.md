# Skill — waveform editing

## Use when

Turning semantic transcript edits into precise audio/video cuts.

## Three-source rule

- Transcript: what should be removed.
- Waveform: where sound actually starts and ends.
- Playback: whether the new human-perceived transition works.

No one source is sufficient by itself.

## Per-cut loop

1. Zoom into both sides of the proposed join.
2. Locate the true phoneme boundaries; silence detection is advisory only.
3. Preserve the entire final sound of the outgoing word and initial sound of the incoming word.
4. Remove unintended breath or micro-pause when it makes the edit feel discontinuous.
5. Ripple close the gap.
6. Compare waveform continuity across the join.
7. Play the join repeatedly, then in sentence context.
8. Reject stutters, doubled sounds, clipped consonants, unnatural cadence, and any gap the brain registers as an edit.
9. Log final source/timecode boundaries.

Visual jump cuts are permitted when audio is clean. Never hide a bad audio edit behind captions or music.
