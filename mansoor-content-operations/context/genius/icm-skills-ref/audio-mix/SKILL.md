# Skill — audio mix

## Use when

Balancing voice and optional music for a finished assembly.

## Method

1. Clean and set the voice first. The tutorial example hovers around -1 to -3 dB while speaking.
2. Decide whether music adds emotion or merely adds noise. Teaching can remain dry.
3. If music is used, choose an instrumental bed whose emotion supports the message.
4. Introduce it below voice. The tutorial's flat track sits roughly -18 to -20 dB.
5. For dynamic music, automate gain/ducking rather than forcing the entire track to one level.
6. Listen on speakers and headphones. Speech must remain effortlessly intelligible.
7. Confirm music does not mask edit joins or create a false emotional claim.

The displayed values are references from one example, not substitutes for listening or loudness policy supplied by a platform.
