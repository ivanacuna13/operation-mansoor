# Skill — caption styling

## Use when

Creating and correcting burned-in captions after the message and headline are approved.

## Required style

- Manrope Regular, size 23, tracking -27.
- Centered, position 70 units down in the demonstrated Premiere setup.
- White with a slight shadow matched visually to the reference.
- Maximum 23 characters per caption segment.
- Minimum duration 2 seconds.
- Exactly one line; double lines forbidden.
- Just below the mouth/around the chin; never cover the mouth or face.
- Remain outside platform red zones.
- Do not display standard captions during the opening headline interval.

## QC loop

1. Regenerate from the engineered audio, not the original transcript.
2. Compare every word and punctuation mark to playback.
3. Check segmentation, timing, line count, and character count.
4. Inspect frames at headline exit and every caption boundary.
5. Correct names, numbers, contractions, and sentence-ending punctuation manually.
