# Mansoor clipper

Clip one Mansoor talking short the way a Premiere editor would. Visual process and clip-edit process both apply.

Open and follow:

Do not invent a new pipeline. Do not use Lifestyle Copycat motion.

## Hard fails

- Picking a moment from a transcript without watching the footage.
- Shipping a file that freezes, is 1 second of picture, or does not play to the end.
- 4K landscape export, square-stack blur, or a crop that ignores the Premiere sit-down.
- Slicing a word. Leaving um / uh / false starts / “now” + pause / repeated sentences.
- Showing Mansoor’s wife’s face. Using the maroon-puffer ski dad as Mansoor.
- Faceless B-roll-only talking shorts. His face must be in the cut.
- Whisper. ElevenLabs first, Parakeet fallback.
- Sales-call PII: names, emails, DOB, bank, address, health, application tail. Strip it.
- Sending a batch. One clip at a time until Ivan says otherwise.
- Telling Ivan a clip is done without watching the export at normal speed.

## Process (every clip)

1. **Watch the source.** Use watchVideo (or sit through the relevant section). Hear what he is actually saying and how he lands it. A transcript is a map, not the cut.
2. **Name the promise.** One breath. Hook that promises value. If you cannot say it, kill the idea.
3. **Name the delivery.** The end is where that promise lands (downward intonation, complete thought). Incomplete examples and extra loops are not an ending.
4. **Engineer the message.** Cut filler, additive setup, repeats, dead air, “so / now / because the principle here is the following.” Remaining words must read as good writing out of context.
5. **Razor on the waveform.** Zoom until you see the gap. Cut at the start of the next word. Ripple delete. Close ~0.1s Premiere “dot” pauses. Play the joins. If the wave is continuous, the viewer will not feel the cut. Visual jump is allowed only if audio flows. If you cannot see a waveform, you cannot ship.
6. **Frame the picture.** Full-screen 9:16, 1080×1920, face fill, scale about 180–184%. Match the Premiere sit-down stills. Not a random 180% crop.
7. **Headline then captions** unless the job says no text.
   - First ~4 seconds: headline only (promise or curiosity gap, does not repeat the verbal hook, max two lines, center, around the chin).
   - Then captions: Manrope Regular, 23, tracking -27, one line, max 23 characters, min 2 seconds, white + slight black shadow, on the chin, not on the mouth, not in red zones.
   - Filename is the headline.
8. **Export.** H.264 mp4, 1080×1920, source frame rate, VBR ~10–12 Mbps, AAC 48 kHz stereo 320 kbps. Must play all the way through. Probe duration and bitrate before send.
9. **Watch the cut.** Full playback at normal speed. Promise plus delivery test. If a join clicks, a word is sliced, picture dies, or the point does not land, recut. Do not send.

## Delivery

Send one playable file. Ivan reviews: ✅ keep, ❌ kill, ✍️ / 📝 recut from his notes only. Do not start the next clip until he reacts or asks.

Default music: none. Teaching can ship silent under the voice.

## Done

A stranger can watch it, get the promise, and never feel a cut. Waveform evidence exists for every ripple. The file plays. Transcript-only QC is not done.
