# Definition of done

A clip is done only when every mandatory item passes.

## Meaning

- The opening contains a recognizable hook: a promise, tension, or curiosity that makes the rest worth watching.
- The ending delivers the promised point and lands on a complete thought, preferably with downward vocal intonation.
- The clip works as a standalone message while remaining faithful to the source's broader context.
- Every retained sentence contributes to the main promise; redundant phrasing, excessive examples, filler, and transition scaffolding are removed.
- Compression preserves logic. Reading the engineered transcript without the removed sections still makes sense.

## Edit integrity

- No word is clipped at the beginning or end.
- Every edit boundary is verified visually on the waveform and audibly in playback.
- No transcript-created micro-gap, breath gap, stutter, doubled consonant, or unnatural pause remains.
- Visual jump cuts are acceptable when the audio is imperceptibly continuous.
- The final cut is reviewed from beginning to end at normal playback speed.

## Headline and captions

- Headline communicates the clip's value proposition through a promise or curiosity gap.
- Headline complements the spoken hook; it does not merely repeat it.
- Any factual claim is supported by source context.
- Headline is center aligned, balanced, readable, inside platform safe zones, near the chin, and no more than two lines.
- Headline appears for roughly the first four seconds.
- Captions do not appear while the headline is doing the same job.
- Captions use the exact style rules in `context/STYLE-SPEC.md`, remain single-line, avoid the face and safe-zone violations, and have perfect wording and punctuation.

## Audio and export

- Voice is dominant and normally hovers around -1 to -3 dB in the tutorial's mixer example.
- Optional music is instrumental, emotionally appropriate, non-overpowering, and normally around -18 to -20 dB in the tutorial's flat-track example.
- Silence is valid when music adds no value.
- Export is H.264, 1080x1920, source/sequence frame rate, progressive, hardware encoded when available, maximum depth and maximum render quality enabled, VBR 1-pass around 10-12 Mbps, AAC 48 kHz stereo at 320 kbps, with captions burned in.
- Export range is exactly the approved in/out range.
- Filename uses the approved headline, filesystem-safe, with `.mp4`.

## Evidence

- Candidate record, approval, cut plan, edit decisions, headline options, assembly manifest, QC report, and final path all exist.
- QC report contains no unresolved mandatory failures.
- A human or fresh agent can trace the final clip back to its source timestamps and decisions.

## Non-goals

There is no arbitrary duration target. Under a minute is useful, but value per second and completeness decide length. Platform limits are constraints, not editorial goals.
