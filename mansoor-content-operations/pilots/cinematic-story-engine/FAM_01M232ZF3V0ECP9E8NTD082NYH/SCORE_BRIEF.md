# Origin Pilot — Custom Instrumental Brief

## Prompt

> Create a 23.5-second instrumental score for a grounded documentary transformation. Begin dry, intimate, and narrow with a distinctive two- or three-note felt-piano motif over subtle room-like texture. At 2 seconds, introduce an unresolved low cello or bowed texture without becoming tragic. From 7.8 seconds, add a restrained organic pulse and one muted percussive layer to support visible work. At 14.4 seconds, create 120–250 milliseconds of negative space, followed by a restrained marker-like impact and harmonic pivot. From 17.6 seconds, widen into warm strings and a clear but modest statement of the opening motif. At 20.2 seconds, remove percussion, resolve the harmony, and leave an airy natural tail through 23.5 seconds. Approximately 76 BPM, 4/4. Intimate modern documentary; patient, tactile, earned, and calm. No vocals, choir, vocal chops, lyrics, trap drums, heroic brass, giant taiko, trailer risers, ostentatious luxury mood, or imitation of any named artist or existing score.

## Creation process

1. Generate several candidates from the same brief.
2. Reject vocals, recognizable imitation, trailer-scale drama, weak turning points, and unusable endings.
3. Shortlist the candidates whose phrases align at 0.0, 2.0, 7.8, 14.4, 17.6, and 20.2 seconds.
4. Choose based on story fit and restraint, not isolated musical impressiveness.
5. Regenerate or rearrange weak sections rather than heavily stretching the final track.
6. Export the highest-quality master and any available musical parts useful for the edit.

## Acceptance test

- recognizable sonic signature within 500 milliseconds;
- no full percussion before 7.8 seconds;
- meaningful contrast at 14.4 seconds without a cliché boom;
- emotional maximum supports authority and meaning rather than hardship;
- no percussion after 20.2 seconds;
- clean musical resolution by approximately 23.1 seconds;
- no vocals or named-artist imitation;
- ending leaves space for natural air and water.
