# 13 - BRYSON TOF/MOF/BOF INTEGRATION STRATEGY

## THE FRAMEWORK

Bryson's content strategy operates on a three-layer funnel: Top of Funnel (TOF), Middle of Funnel (MOF), and Bottom of Funnel (BOF). Each layer has a specific job, a specific content type, and a specific metric. The layers work together as a system. Most insurance creators only run TOF. They post content, get views, and never convert because there is no MOF or BOF behind it.

For Mansoor's channel, the TOF/MOF/BOF system maps onto the two ICPs and the Marczell integration matrix. TOF attracts both ICPs using pain-first hooks. MOF separates them and warms them with ICP-specific content. BOF drives them to the offer: a booked call or a direct-to-checkout training.

---

## TOP OF FUNNEL (TOF): ATTRACT

### Job
Reach cold audiences who match either ICP profile. Make them stop scrolling. Make them feel understood. Do not sell. Do not pitch. Make them think "this guy gets me."

### Content Type
Short-form video, 30-60 seconds. Pain-first hooks. No mention of the offer. No CTA to book a call. The only CTA is implicit: follow for more.

### Hook Formula
Open with the ICP's specific pain in their own language. For ICP 1: "You have 200 agents on your team and you are making $30K a month." For ICP 2: "You are closing $25K a month and you are working 80 hours a week to do it." The hook does not teach. It names the pain so precisely that the viewer feels seen.

### Content Mix at TOF
60-70% pure insurance strategy and sales training. These are the videos that prove competence. "Here is how to handle the top 3 objections on a final expense call." "Here is how to structure your week as a recruiter." "Here is why your persistency is dropping and the 3 things that fix it."

30-40% Marczell-derived concepts from the Translation Matrix. These are the differentiator videos. "You hit a week of chargebacks and you stopped dialing. That is not a sales problem. That is an emotional noise problem. Here is the fix." The viewer has never heard this from an insurance creator. That is the hook.

### TOF Metrics
Views, watch time, follower growth, saves, shares. Do not measure TOF by leads or sales. TOF is attention capture. If you measure it by revenue, you will kill it before it compounds.

### Volume
15 TOF videos per month minimum. 20 is better. The algorithm needs volume to find your audience. 5 videos a month is not a strategy. It is a hobby.

### Audience Targeting
Broad. Do not narrow the audience with interest targeting. Let the content do the targeting. The hook is the filter. If the hook names the ICP pain precisely, the algorithm finds the ICP. If the hook is generic, the algorithm finds everyone and converts no one.

## MIDDLE OF FUNNEL (MOF): SEPARATE AND WARM

### Job
Take the TOF audience and separate them by ICP. Warm them with ICP-specific content that builds trust and desire. Move them from "this guy gets me" to "I need to talk to this guy."

### Content Type
Longer-form content. YouTube videos (8-15 minutes), Instagram carousels, email sequences. The content goes deeper than TOF. It is not a hook. It is a full argument. It takes the pain named in TOF and delivers the mechanism.

### The Separation Mechanism
At MOF, you create content that speaks to one ICP at a time explicitly. A TOF video says "you are stuck at $30K a month." A MOF video says "if you are a recruiter with a big team and no sales training, this is for you." The recruiter leans in. The lone wolf scrolls past. That is the separation. You are not excluding. You are letting the audience self-select.

### MOF Content for ICP 1 (Recruiter)
Deep-dive content on sales training for teams, compensation model restructuring, the difference between production and take-home income, the Leverage Matrix (container for sales training), the Identity Inversion (you are a scaler currently recruiting). The content proves that Mansoor understands the recruiter's specific trap and has a specific way out.

### MOF Content for ICP 2 (Lone Wolf)
Deep-dive content on duplication, carbon copies, selling in front of an audience, the Container Method (recruiting wrapped in client acquisition language), the Timeline Compress (how to go from 5-year freedom plan to 6-month freedom plan). The content proves that Mansoor understands the lone wolf's specific trap and has a specific way out.

### MOF Metrics
Engagement rate, comment quality, DM volume, email open rate, click-through rate on content links. The signal you are looking for: people asking questions in comments and DMs. Questions mean interest. Interest means the MOF is working.

### The MOF Asset
Create one core MOF asset per ICP. For ICP 1: a free training video or PDF titled "The Sales-Trained Team Model: How 60 Agents Outproduce 300." For ICP 2: a free training video or PDF titled "The Carbon Copy System: How to Stop Selling and Start Scaling." These assets are the bridge from MOF to BOF. They are the thing the prospect consumes right before they decide to book a call.

### Email Sequence Design
The MOF email sequence is 5-7 emails over 10 days. Each email delivers one concept from the Translation Matrix in written form. Not a pitch. A realization. Email 1: The Identity Inversion. Email 2: The State-Revenue Connection. Email 3: The Derailment Pattern. Email 4: The Resonance Audit. Email 5: The Container Method. Email 6: The Leverage Matrix. Email 7: The call to action. Each email is 300-500 words. Value dense. No fluff. The prospect should feel like they got a free coaching session, not a sales sequence.

## BOTTOM OF FUNNEL (BOF): CONVERT

### Job
Take the warmed MOF audience and drive them to the offer. The offer is a booked call. The BOF content is not educational. It is decisional. It helps the prospect make the decision, not gather more information.

### Content Type
Direct response. Short-form videos with explicit CTAs. Retargeting ads. Sales pages. Application forms. Calendar links. The BOF content says "here is what I do, here is who it is for, here is how to start."

### BOF Content for ICP 1
A direct-response video or page that says: "If you have a team of 50+ agents and you are stuck at $30K/month because your team cannot sell, I can fix that in 90 days. Here is how: I install a sales training system into your existing team. You do not need to recruit anyone new. You need to train the people you have. Book a call and I will show you exactly what your team is missing."

### BOF Content for ICP 2
A direct-response video or page that says: "If you are closing $25K+ a month, working 60-80 hours a week, and you have never built a team because recruiting feels like an MLM scam, I can fix that in 90 days. I teach you to sell in front of an audience and turn every close into a training opportunity. You do not need to recruit. You need to duplicate. Book a call. I will show you exactly how to build your first carbon copy. Link is below."

### The BOF Retargeting System
Run retargeting ads to everyone who watched 50%+ of a MOF video, engaged with a MOF email, or visited the MOF asset page. The retargeting creative is short, direct, and testimonial-driven. "Here is a recruiter who went from $30K to $80K in 90 days by installing sales training into his existing team." "Here is a lone wolf who went from 80 hours a week to 30 by building 3 carbon copies." The retargeting frequency should be 3-5 impressions per week. Not higher. You are not hammering. You are reminding.

### BOF Metrics
Cost per booked call, show rate, close rate, AOV. These are the only metrics that matter at BOF. If TOF and MOF are working, BOF is a math problem. If they are not, BOF is a leak.

### The Application Funnel
The BOF conversion mechanism is an application funnel, not a direct booking link. The application filters for qualified prospects. Questions: current monthly income, team size (for ICP 1) or personal production (for ICP 2), biggest bottleneck, timeline to scale. The application does two things: it qualifies the prospect, and it gives Mansoor the context he needs to close on the call. The application is the last MOF asset and the first BOF asset. It bridges the two.

## THE FULL SYSTEM MAP

Week 1-2: TOF content goes live. 15-20 short-form videos. Pain-first hooks. No CTA. Pure attention capture.

Week 3-4: MOF content goes live. 2 long-form videos (one per ICP). Email sequence launches to anyone who engaged with TOF. MOF asset pages go live.

Week 5-6: BOF content goes live. Direct-response videos with explicit CTAs. Retargeting campaigns launch to MOF engagers. Application funnel opens.

Week 7+: The system runs as a loop. TOF feeds MOF. MOF feeds BOF. BOF feeds the call calendar. Every week, new TOF content enters the top. Every week, warmed prospects exit the bottom as booked calls.

### Content Volume Per Month
TOF: 15-20 short-form videos
MOF: 4-6 long-form videos or carousels (2-3 per ICP)
BOF: 4-6 direct-response videos or ads (2-3 per ICP)
Email: 20 emails (5 per week, 4 weeks)

### The Marczell Dosage Across the Full System
TOF: 30-40% of videos use a Marczell-derived concept as the core mechanism
MOF: 40-50% of deep-dive content uses a Marczell-derived concept (more room here because the format is longer)
BOF: 10-15% of direct-response content uses a Marczell-derived concept (BOF is about the offer, not the philosophy)

This dosage distribution keeps the Marczell influence heaviest in MOF where the prospect is already engaged and has appetite for depth, and lightest in BOF where the prospect needs a decision, not a realization.

## TOF SCRIPT TEMPLATES

### Template A: Pure Insurance Strategy (60-70% of TOF)

Hook (0-3 seconds): Name the ICP pain in their language.
Body (3-45 seconds): Deliver one tactical insight. No theory. No philosophy. One thing they can do today.
Close (45-60 seconds): Land the insight. No CTA. Let the value be the CTA.

Example for ICP 1:
"You have 200 agents on your team and you are making $30K a month. Here is why. 140 of your agents produce under $500 a month. You are managing 200 people to get the production of 60. Cut the 140. Train the 60. Your income doubles and your workload drops by 70%. You do not have a recruiting problem. You have a tolerance problem."

Example for ICP 2:
"You are closing $25K a month and working 80 hours a week. Here is why. Every close is a one-to-one transaction. You sell one policy to one person. If you recorded your best presentation and trained one person to deliver it, you would have two closers. If you trained three, you would have four closers. Your income would double and your hours would halve. You do not have a sales problem. You have a duplication problem."

### Template B: Marczell-Derived Concept (30-40% of TOF)

Hook (0-3 seconds): Name the ICP pain in their language.
Body (3-45 seconds): Deliver the concept as the mechanism. Use the insurance container name. Ground it in a concrete insurance scenario.
Close (45-60 seconds): Land the concept. No CTA. Let the realization be the CTA.

Example for ICP 1:
"You have a big team and you are stuck at $30K. You keep trying to recruit more people. That is the wrong move. You are not a recruiter who might scale. You are a scaler who is currently recruiting. The difference changes everything. A scaler looks at the 200 agents and asks: which 60 of these would my future self have recruited? A recruiter looks at the 200 and asks: how do I get to 300? The scaler cuts 140 and doubles income. The recruiter adds 100 and stays at $30K."

Example for ICP 2:
"You are grinding 80 hours a week and making $25K. You think the problem is that you need more leads. It is not. The problem is emotional noise. You had a bad persistency week and the noise started. 'These leads are garbage. This carrier is ripping me off.' You stopped dialing for 3 days. Those 3 days had 15 appointments in them. 4 would have closed. That is $8K you left on the table. The persistency drop was $3K. The noise cost $8K. The noise is always more expensive than the event."

## THE EMAIL SEQUENCE (MOF BRIDGE)

### Email 1: The Identity Inversion (Day 1)
Subject: You are not who you think you are
Open with the pain. Deliver the Identity Inversion concept in 400 words. Close with a question that makes them reply. "Which identity are you operating from right now? Reply and let me know."

### Email 2: The State-Revenue Connection (Day 3)
Subject: The hidden variable killing your production
Deliver the State-Revenue Connection. Show how emotional state drives pipeline. Close with a question. "When was the last time a bad week cost you more in pipeline than the actual event? Reply with the number."

### Email 3: The Derailment Pattern (Day 5)
Subject: The noise is more expensive than the event
Deliver the Derailment Pattern. Show how emotional noise costs more than the triggering event. Close with a question. "What is the noise costing you right now? Reply with the dollar amount."

### Email 4: The Resonance Audit (Day 7)
Subject: You are working on the wrong thing
Deliver the Resonance Audit. Show how drifting from the original goal creates fatigue. Close with a question. "What did you start this for? Has your week drifted from that? Reply with your original goal."

### Email 5: The Container Method (Day 8)
Subject: How to sell without anyone knowing you are selling
Deliver the Container Method. Show how the container changes how content is received. Close with a question. "What are you trying to deliver that keeps getting rejected? Reply with the container you are using."

### Email 6: The Leverage Matrix (Day 9)
Subject: Stop doing 1-to-1 work
Deliver the Leverage Matrix. Show how to convert 1-to-1 into 1-to-many. Close with a question. "What is the one activity eating your week that could be recorded and deployed? Reply with it."

### Email 7: The Call to Action (Day 10)
Subject: Let's look at your numbers together
No concept. Direct CTA. "If you have been reading these emails and nodding, it is time to talk. Book a call. I will look at your team, your production, and your bottleneck, and tell you exactly what to fix first. Link is below."

## INTEGRATION NOTES

The Marczell concepts are the differentiator. They are what make Mansoor's content unlike anything else in the insurance space. No other insurance creator is teaching identity architecture, emotional noise, or the container technique. That is the moat.

The Bryson TOF/MOF/BOF structure is the delivery system. It is what turns the concepts into a funnel that converts. Without the three-layer structure, the concepts are just content. With it, they are a customer acquisition system.

The two ICPs are the targeting mechanism. They ensure every piece of content speaks to a specific person with a specific pain. Generic content converts no one. ICP-specific content converts the right people.

The dosage rules prevent overdilution. 30-40% Marczell at TOF. 40-50% at MOF. 10-15% at BOF. This keeps the Marczell influence present but never dominant. The insurance strategy is always the primary content. The Marczell concepts are the mechanism that makes the strategy land differently than every other insurance creator.

The system compounds. TOF builds the audience. MOF builds the trust. BOF builds the calendar. Each layer feeds the next. By month 3, the system runs on autopilot: new TOF content enters the top, warmed prospects exit the bottom as booked calls, and the only manual work is creating the content and taking the calls.
